What you're looking at

Every action a bot takes is written to a hash-chained audit log before it executes, and each entry commits to the hash of the one before it. “Sealed” here means the whole chain re-verified from genesis: no gaps, no altered records. If even one entry were edited, the state on this page would flip to verification failed instead of staying quiet.
