An AI workforce you can actually let near your systems.

Three guarantees, not vibes

01 Decided before. Recorded after.

Nothing a bot does is implicit. Each action passes a fail-closed policy engine first — allow, deny, or park it for human approval. The decision is written to a tamper-evident, hash-chained audit log before anything executes, so refusals and crashes are on the record too. Secrets never land in the log — only their length.

02 A computer per bot. Not one for all.

Bots don't share a desktop or your sign-ins. Each bot works in its own isolated computer session — its own browser profile, its own file space, its own tool access. What one bot does can't contaminate another, and your own sign-ins stay yours.

03 Model routing with fallback.

Each bot's brain is configuration, not identity. Route different bots to different model providers, and when a provider degrades, a verified fallback chain keeps the workforce running. You change the model — never the authority, the memory, or the audit trail.
